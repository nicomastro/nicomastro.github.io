Bronchoscopy Research

This research focuses on developing methods for identifying anatomical positions during broncho-endoscopic navigation. Together with Professor Daniel Acevedo and Professor Viviana Cotik at Instituto de Ciencias de la Computación (CONICET-UBA), we collaborate with the Unidad de Terapia Intensiva, Hospital Italiano de Buenos Aires, Argentina and Instituto de Medicina Traslacional e Ingeniería Biomédica (IMTIB), Argentina. I co-supervise Master's students working on related topics, including Jorge Szabo, with whom I am currently developing spatio-temporal deep learning methods with anatomical constraints for bronchial segment identification.

AI-assisted bronchoscopy in the intensive care unit: Corpus development and an application to anatomic position identifications
IBERAMIA 2024 (Oral)
PAPER
This study aims to create a model capable of accurately identifying bronchial segments during broncho-endoscopic navigation. To achieve this, we analyzed 126 videos from bronchoscopic procedures conducted on critically ill patients at a university hospital in Buenos Aires, Argentina. A dataset of consistently annotated videos, captured by bronchoscopists with varied expertise, was established. This paper presents the annotation schema, labeling guidelines, the developed corpus, and some preliminary results.
Luciano Tarsia, Nicolas Mastropasqua, Indalecio Carboni Bisso, Marcos Las Heras, Marcelo Risk, María Florencia Courtois, Ignacio Fernández Ceballos, Carolina Lockhart, Daniel Acevedo, Viviana Cotik
